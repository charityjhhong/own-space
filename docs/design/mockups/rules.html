Shared rules for the five tabs

One typeface, four text styles, one spacing scale, one set of sizes and states, and the wording rules every mockup follows.

What changes
  1. One typeface and only four text styles: 14 semibold, 14 regular, 12 semibold, 12 regular.
  2. Boxes only where something is pressable or truly groups things (list of removals below).
  3. Wording has six short rules; the table of every changed label is kept apart for approval.

Orange cells are estimates or open decisions.

Wording rules

RuleSays
W1 CaseSentence case everywhere. No ALL CAPS.
W2 ButtonsStart with a verb, at most 3 words, no pronouns (this, the real one). No full stop.
W3 Names and captionsSection names are 1 to 3 words. Captions are at most 8 words and fit one line. Neither ends with a full stop.
W4 MessagesInstructions, warnings and toasts are full sentences and end with a full stop.
W5 One word per thingzone = the part that stays real (My zone); outside = the virtual rest; room = the physical room; space = a saved space only; cover = a Tidy up cover; design = a finish or furniture choice.
W6 SymbolsNo ellipsis, no middle dot in labels (use a comma). One name per tab: Style, My room, Add, AI, Tidy up.
