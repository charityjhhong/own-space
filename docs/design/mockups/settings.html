Settings list

The Settings sheet, redrawn with the app's real glyphs and one type set. The top strip and tab row are the shared shell; the gear is lit and the header has no ×.

What changes
  1. Gear is the app's own glyph, and there are no divider lines or pill behind Light | Dark.
  2. One width and one edge for every button on the right; all captions fit on one line (wording changes are on the rules page).
  3. Fits the 460 mm panel: 408 mm in release builds, 448 mm in dev builds with Real occlusion under Design.

Wording is the proposed wording; the table of every change, current → proposed, is on the rules page (the human approves it separately).
