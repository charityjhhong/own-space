# Demo video: shot list (P6)

Plan: **2:55** (hard limit 3:00), public on YouTube, English captions, no AI-generated video. Filmed in a real dorm room, recorded on a Quest 3. Follows `CONCEPT.md` section 6. Replaces the times in `storyboard.md` (which stays as the idea).

Words: *pinch* = point the hand ray at it and pinch (or poke it with a finger when close). *Panel* = the glass panel beside your right hand. Tabs: **Style, My room, Add, AI, Tidy up**. Gear = Settings, next to the minimize bar.

## Before you film (10 minutes)

1. Install the build you will submit; note its version for every take. Battery over 60%, Wi-Fi on (the AI needs a network), headset updated.
2. Run Space Setup in the dorm once; set the zone around your desk, chair and bed side (zone card: drag the corners, pinch **Done**).
3. Put a laptop open on the real desk. Screen: a neutral document, no private information. Tidy the room except a small pile of laundry on the floor (for Tidy up).
4. **Save the room:** gear › Room › Current space › **Change** › **Save this room** › **Home**. (Used in the Saved spaces shot.)
5. Start from a clean design: gear › Design › **Reset** › **Confirm**. Log in to YouTube in the headset Browser beforehand, pick a calm video (no ads, no private account details), and pair the laptop with Meta's remote desktop once.
6. Check the desk's real height with a tape measure once (it must match the redesign).
7. Both people in the room agree to be filmed. Hands visible in every headset shot; controllers put away.

## How to record on the Quest **[confirm each on the device]**

- **Best: the Meta camera on the headset.** Press the **Meta (Oculus) button**, open the **camera / Sharing** shortcut, choose **Record video**, start; press the same shortcut to stop. This captures passthrough, the virtual room and your hands in one clip. Do one recording per shot (10 to 40 s), not one long take; keep the app open between shots.
- **Backup: cast and record.** Meta Quest phone app › **Cast** to the phone or a laptop, then screen-record there (lower quality, lagging). Or Meta Quest Developer Hub › Device Manager › capture.
- **Screenshots** (also for Devpost): the same Meta camera shortcut › **Take photo**, or from the Mac `adb shell am startservice -n com.oculus.metacam/.capture.CaptureService -a TAKE_SCREENSHOT` and pull from `/sdcard/Oculus/Screenshots`.
- **The wide room shots** (before and after) are filmed with a normal phone from the same tripod mark, not on the headset.
- Retake any shot with a visible controller, a private screen, or a frozen app. Do not use AI video.

## If a shot cannot be filmed

- **No second place for Saved spaces:** use the built-in sample room: gear › Room › Current space › **Change** › **Sample room**. Caption "A saved room, anywhere."
- **No network for the AI:** pinch a style chip (**Living room**) instead of typing; caption "Pick a style. The AI furnishes the room." Film the typed request twice if the Wi-Fi is good.
- **Anything not in the submitted build** (the guided first run): cut the shot and give its seconds to the core-use shot (1:34-2:10).
- **The Meta menu windows do not float over Own Space** or a window blocks the view: film the laptop and YouTube on the real desk instead, with the room behind, and say so only if shown. The video must show only what the build does.
- Never show Real occlusion, voice, or a hand that cuts through the virtual room.

## Editing notes

- English captions burned in or as a subtitle track. No narration needed; music only if CC0 or licensed (record it in `docs/design/asset-licenses.md`).
- The first 15 seconds do not depend on the panel layout beyond **My room** and **Keep real top**. Film them first and last (if it fails, redo it).
- Export 1080p or higher, 2:55 or less; set the YouTube video **public** and check the link while logged out.
